FAQ

Questions agents ask before switching.

What is DV Agent OS?

A ready-made system for Dream Valley Realty agents that captures leads, replies to them in under a minute, follows up automatically and keeps past clients in touch, all in one place.

How long does setup take?

Everything is pre-built. We connect your phone number, calendar and branding, and you're live once that's done.

Will the AI talk to my leads without me knowing?

The AI assistant only replies to your DV leads, saves every answer to the contact and alerts you when a lead wants a person. Any reply from you pauses it for that conversation.

Is it compliant with Canadian anti-spam rules?

Yes. Forms collect consent, texts only go to people who agreed, and STOP replies are honoured automatically.

Can I keep my existing contacts?

Yes. Import them, and the reactivation campaign checks consent before reaching out.
